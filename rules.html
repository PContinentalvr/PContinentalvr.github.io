THE HOUSE CODE

A civil word
is always in fashion.

A short guide to keeping this VRChat house welcoming, safe, and good company.

TC / CODE
✳

Neutral ground, kindly kept.

Here, the old hotel custom means something simple: no drama, no harassment, and room for everyone to feel safe. The atmosphere is theatrical; our care for one another is real.

01

Courtesy is the house style.

Speak with respect. Disagree without making a guest feel unwelcome. Slurs, harassment, dogpiling, and repeated unwanted contact are not admitted.

02

Ask before you cross a boundary.

Respect personal space, consent, and a clear no. Do not pressure anyone for personal details, images, or private conversation. Keep other guests' confidences off the public board.

03

Keep the fiction in the fiction.

Coins, markers, titles, and the house's grand manner are playful community flavour. They are not debts, authority over another guest, or permission to threaten or intimidate.

04

Finery is welcome; never required.

A little elegance suits the house, should you fancy it. Your avatar, style, comfort, and accessibility needs come first. Come as yourself.

05

The Manager keeps the peace.

If a house rule is broken, staff may step in, ask for a change, pause access, or remove someone when needed to keep guests safe. The joking word excommunication means only that a guest may be barred from the community; no public shaming or spectacle.

06

A coin is a keepsake, not a price.

Gold coins recognise active participation in the community. They have no real-world cash value. A marker is a friendly promise between members: never a binding debt, and always something one may decline.

Need the front desk?

Contact the house team privately on Discord if something feels wrong or you need help. You need not confront anyone yourself.

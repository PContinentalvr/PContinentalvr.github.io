A NOTE ON THE FICTION

The order of
the High Table.

A plain-language guide to the fictional underworld's titles—and where our friendly VRChat house is different.

TC / ORDER
✳

A fictional order, not our org chart.

The films present a secret society with its own customs and a governing council called the High Table. Details shift between stories; this is a broad guide, not a claim that every title forms one tidy chain of command.

  1. I

    Above the table · The Elder

    In one part of the story, the Elder is presented as an authority above the High Table. This position is tied to the films' changing events, not a permanent office in our community.

  2. II

    The council · The High Table

    A council representing powerful underworld organisations. Its members and reach sit at the top of the fictional system; it is not a real-world institution.

  3. III

    The seats · Organisation leaders

    Each seat represents a major organisation or family. A seat may pass through a family or political arrangement in the stories; there is no need to treat that as a universal rule.

APPOINTED FUNCTIONS, NOT RANKS

Delegated offices · Adjudicators & Harbingers

Adjudicators carry out the Table's instructions and assess a house's conduct. Harbingers formally deliver the Table's terms and decisions. The Marquis de Gramont is appointed to pursue the Table's judgement in Chapter 4. These are delegated offices, not permanent steps in a universal ladder.

NETWORKS & WORKING ROLES

Power does not all sit at the Table.

The Director leads the Ruska Roma, an organisation with its own traditions. The Bowery King commands an independent network of informants. Assassins, trackers, bounty hunters, and other associates do the work of the underworld; they are callings or affiliations, not rungs below the council.

THE HOTEL, FROM MANAGER TO NIGHT STAFF

The Continental's house staff

Each hotel is run by its Manager, with a Concierge and front desk receiving guests. The films also show or refer to specialist and service staff. The complete list below is a practical roleplay roster, not a claim that every post appears on screen or ranks beneath another.

  • ManagerOversees the hotel and upholds its house customs.
  • ConciergeThe guest's trusted point of contact and guide to the house.
  • Front desk & receptionChecks guests in, keeps the ledger, and answers enquiries.
  • Switchboard operatorsConnect calls and pass messages discreetly.
  • SommelierAdvises guests and tends the hotel's cellar.
  • TailorFits formal wear and specialist attire.
  • DoctorProvides discreet medical care to guests.
  • Cleaners & housekeepingRestore rooms and keep the hotel in order.
  • BartenderKeeps the bar and its quiet conversations in good order.
  • Porters & door staffWelcome arrivals and assist guests at the entrance.
✳

Coins & markers · Customs, not cash

In the films, gold coins signal access to the underworld's services, while a marker records a personal pledge. Their story-world meaning is fictional. Our community coins are a participation perk with no cash value; our markers are friendly and optional.

OUR HOUSEHOLD RP

Our house, in roleplay.

This is our community's roleplay chain, inspired by the films. Nekozushka is the Elder, full-time owner, and final authority in the RP. Succubi is the Manager and community moderator. Prickett is the Concierge, knows the house inside out, and usually welcomes new guests and staff.

  1. I

    The Elder · Nekozushka

    Full-time owner and highest authority in this roleplay. The final word is his.

  2. II

    Manager · Succubi

    The community's moderator / administrator, responsible for helping manage the house.

  3. III

    Concierge · Prickett

    Knows the house inside out and usually receives newcomers, from first-time guests to new staff.

The other hotel posts above are roles for our Continental RP. Their titles add flavour and scenes; they do not grant moderation or owner authority.
